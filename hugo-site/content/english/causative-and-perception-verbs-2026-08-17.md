## 📖 오늘의 문법: 사역동사 & 지각동사 (Causative & Perception Verbs)

- make / have / let + 동사원형 → *My boss made me redo it.*
- get + to부정사 → *I got him to apologize.* (설득 뉘앙스)
- 목적어가 당하는 입장이면 p.p. → *I had my car fixed.*
- 지각동사 see/hear/watch/feel + 원형(전체) vs -ing(진행 중) → *saw him cross / crossing*
- 수동태가 되면 to가 부활 → *I was made to wait.* (let은 대신 be allowed to)

> 💡 **Tip:** 사역·지각동사는 "목적어가 하는가, 당하는가"만 물어보세요. 하면 → 원형/-ing (have him check), 당하면 → p.p. (have it checked).

### 예문 5가지

1. **She made me redo the entire presentation.**
2. **I had the technician fix my laptop yesterday.**
3. **I had my hair cut before the interview.**
4. **He got his colleague to review the contract.**
5. **My parents let me borrow the car for the trip.**
