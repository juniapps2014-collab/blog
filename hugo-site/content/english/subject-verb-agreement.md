## 📖 오늘의 문법: 주어-동사 수 일치 (Subject-Verb Agreement)

- **each / every + 단수명사** → 항상 단수 동사: *Each student has a laptop.*
- **one of the + 복수명사** → 동사는 단수: *One of the reasons is cost.*
- **neither...nor / either...or** → 동사는 **가까운 주어**에 맞춤: *Neither he nor they were happy.*
- **everyone / everybody / nobody** 등 부정대명사 → 단수 취급: *Everyone is here.*
- **집합명사(team, family, staff)** → 미국식 영어에서는 보통 단수 취급: *The team is winning.*
- **the number of** (단수) vs **a number of** (복수): *The number of errors has dropped.* / *A number of errors have been found.*

> 💡 **Tip:** 헷갈릴 땐 주어와 동사 사이에 낀 수식어(of the students, along with the team 등)는 무시하고, 진짜 주어만 골라내서 동사를 맞추세요.

### 예문 5가지

1. **Each of the students has submitted the report.**
2. **The number of applicants has increased this year.**
3. **Neither the manager nor the employees were informed.**
4. **Everyone is responsible for their own tasks.**
5. **The team is meeting at 3 PM today.**
