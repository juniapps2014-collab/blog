## 📖 오늘의 문법: Second Conditional (가정법 과거) — "만약 ~라면, ~할 텐데"

공식: If + 주어 + 과거형 동사, 주어 + would/could/might + 동사원형

현재 사실과 반대되거나 가능성이 낮은 상황을 가정할 때 사용합니다. be동사는 보통 were를 씁니다.

### 예문 5가지

1. **If I had more time, I would learn to play the guitar.**
2. **If she were here, she would know what to do.**
3. **What would you do if you won the lottery?**
4. **If I were you, I wouldn't send that email yet.**
5. **If our team had a bigger budget, we could hire two more engineers.**
