## 📖 오늘의 문법: Despite / Although / Even Though (양보의 표현)

- **although, though, even though** → 뒤에 **주어 + 동사(절, clause)**가 온다: *Although it was raining, we went out.*
- **despite, in spite of** → 뒤에 **명사, 대명사, 동명사(-ing)**가 온다: *Despite the rain, we went out.* / *Despite being tired, she kept working.*
- **even though**는 **although**보다 의미가 더 강하고 놀라움·의외성을 강조할 때 자주 쓰임: *Even though he studied all night, he failed the test.*
- **though**는 **although**와 거의 같은 뜻이지만 더 캐주얼하고, 문장 맨 뒤에 단독으로도 쓸 수 있음: *I like the plan. I have some concerns, though.*
- 흔한 실수: **despite** 뒤에 바로 절(주어+동사)을 붙이는 것 → ~~Despite he was tired~~ (X) → *Despite being tired* / *Although he was tired* (O)

> 💡 **Tip:** "절이 오면 although/though/even though, 명사·-ing가 오면 despite/in spite of" — 뒤에 오는 게 문장이냐 명사냐만 확인하면 됩니다.

### 예문 5가지

1. **Although the meeting ran late, everyone stayed focused.**
2. **Despite the tight deadline, the team delivered the project on time.**
3. **Even though she had no experience, she got the job.**
4. **In spite of his objections, the plan moved forward.**
5. **I'm happy with the result. It took longer than expected, though.**
